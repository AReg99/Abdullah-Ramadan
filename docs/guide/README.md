## Before you start / قبل البدء

### Signing in / تسجيل الدخول

Everyone signs in with **a number and a password**. The sign-in screen has two
sections — **Owner**, who may also use the email the account was created with,
and **Employees**, who use their phone number. The owner sets everyone's
password in Setup → Staff. Type the number the way you say it (01xxxxxxxxx); the
app recognises it in any form.

الكل بيدخل بـ **رقم وكلمة سر**. شاشة الدخول فيها قسمين — **المالك**، ويقدر كمان
يدخل بالبريد الإلكتروني، و**الموظفين** برقم الموبايل. المالك بيعمل كلمات السر من
الإعدادات ← الموظفين. اكتب الرقم زي ما بتقوله (01xxxxxxxxx).

You can change your own password any time: **حسابي** at the top of the screen.

### Finding your screens / إزاي تلاقي الشاشات

The bar at the bottom shows **five buttons**: the four you use every day, and
**المزيد**, which opens everything else you are allowed to open, grouped by part
of the business. If your job has five screens or fewer, they are all on the bar
and there is no المزيد button.

**A red number on a button means somebody is waiting on you.** Approvals waiting
for a decision, prices that moved, questions from the factory. That is the whole
reason the number is there — open it.

الشريط تحت فيه **خمس أزرار**: الأربعة بتوع كل يوم، و**المزيد** اللي بيفتح باقي
الشاشات مرتبة حسب قسم الشغل. **الرقم الأحمر معناه في حد مستنيك.**

### Changing the language / تغيير اللغة

The `ع | EN` button is in the top corner of every screen. It changes instantly
and remembers your choice.

### If there is no internet / لو مفيش إنترنت

The worker and driver screens keep working. What you do is saved on the phone
and sent automatically when the connection comes back. A small number beside the
sync icon says how many actions are waiting.

**Never stop working because of it.** **متوقفش شغلك عشان النت.**

## The three rules the whole system rests on

### 1. Scan the label at every stage — starting and finishing

Everything the owner sees, every report, every date given to a customer and
every crew's output is built from those scans. **A stage that is worked but not
scanned is invisible to the entire company.**

**امسح الملصق في كل مرحلة — أول ما تبدأ وأول ما تخلّص.** المرحلة اللي بتتعمل من
غير مسح، الشركة كلها مش شايفاها.

### 2. Nothing is deleted — it is reversed

A wrong payment, a wrong stock movement, a wrong invoice: none of them are
erased. You write the opposite entry beside it and both stay visible. That is
what makes the books worth arguing from. The button says **عكس القيد** and it
always asks why.

**مفيش حاجة بتتمسح — بتتعكس.** القيد الغلط بيتكتب جنبه عكسه، والاتنين بيفضلوا
ظاهرين.

### 3. Ask — never guess

If the job card does not say what colour, what fabric, which way round: there is
a button on the card that asks the showroom, and the answer comes back onto the
same card. **A guess about a colour is a piece made twice.**

**اسأل — متخمّنش.** في زرار على كارت الشغل بيسأل المعرض، والرد بيرجع على نفس
الكارت. **التخمين في اللون معناه القطعة تتعمل مرتين.**
